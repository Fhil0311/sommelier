# Code of conduct

Sommelier is made by people who like games and Macs, for others who do. Everyone taking part in its issues, pull
requests, Discord and other spaces is expected to:

- be kind and patient, with newcomers above all; assume good intent;
- criticise ideas and code, never people;
- keep discussion on the project; no harassment, slurs, sexual content, personal attacks or doxxing;
- respect people's time and privacy: don't post someone's personal data, and don't ask for logins or passwords.

Maintainers may edit or remove what breaks this, and block those who keep breaking it. Report a problem privately to
support@playsommelier.com; reports are handled in confidence.

This code is inspired by the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
